Case 01 In production

FastLoad — a platform for the IS Logistics Solutions dispatch team

Client
IS Logistics Solutions LLC
Industry
Logistics · USA
Team
8 dispatchers, 40 drivers

The problem

Dispatchers watched the Sylectus load board by hand (a US marketplace where brokers post freight): refreshing the page, checking every load against each driver's location and truck type, and writing bids to brokers manually. Good loads are gone within minutes, and anything posted outside working hours was simply lost.

What we built

  • 24/7 load board monitoring. The system pulls new loads on its own and matches them to each driver — by area and radius, truck type, dates and mileage limit — and instantly works out the deadhead miles to pickup.
  • Instant Telegram alerts. A matching load reaches the driver and the dispatcher right after it's posted.
  • One-click bids to brokers. The email goes out from the driver's or the company's Gmail with a ready signature and truck details (Gmail access passed Google's verification).
  • Driver portal: their loads, profile, working areas, Gmail and Telegram connection.
  • Bulk SMS panel with AI-varied texts — every message is unique, so fewer land in spam.

Case 02 In production

Workshop management system for 4Power Club

Client
4Power Club
Industry
Car service · Chișinău
Team
6 bays, mechanics, 4 service advisors, warehouse

The problem

Information got lost between people: an advisor didn't learn a car was ready if a colleague had checked it in; cars waiting 2–3 weeks for ordered parts were forgotten; the warehouse received parts but told no one; parts ordered for a car that wasn't on site lost their link to it; customers were told their car was ready by hand. Parts were tracked in 1C.

What we built

  • Real-time car board — by day and by status column; urgent cars are highlighted, and you can see how many days each one has been waiting.
  • Parts tracked through the full cycle “car waiting → ordered → in stock → fitted”, plus a separate tab for parts that arrived without a linked car.
  • 1C sync — warehouse receipts come in automatically, with no double entry.
  • “Shop floor screen” — a shared monitor for the mechanics, nothing to install.
  • Thresholds and working hours — the system raises the alarm by itself when a car or a part is stuck longer than it should be.
  • Notifications to staff in Telegram and automatic messages to customers when their car is ready.
  • A daily summary for the owner.
  • Search by plate number, VIN, phone, surname or work order number; staff roles and access rights.
