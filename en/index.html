Case 01 In production

FastLoad — a platform for the MIRTEH GRUP dispatch team

Client
MIRTEH GRUP
Industry
Logistics · USA
Team
17 dispatchers, 98 drivers

The problem

Dispatchers watched the Sylectus load board by hand (a US marketplace where brokers post freight): refreshing the page, checking every load against each driver's location and truck type, and writing bids to brokers manually. Good loads are gone within minutes, and anything posted outside working hours was simply lost.

What we built

  • 24/7 load board monitoring. The system pulls new loads on its own and matches them to each driver — by area and radius, truck type, dates and mileage limit — and instantly works out the deadhead miles to pickup.
  • Instant Telegram alerts. A matching load reaches the dispatcher right after it's posted — already matched to a specific driver.
  • One-click bids to brokers. The email goes out from the driver's or the company's Gmail with a ready signature and truck details (Gmail access passed Google's verification).
  • Driver portal: their loads, profile, working areas, Gmail and Telegram connection.
  • Bulk SMS panel with AI-varied texts — every message is unique, so fewer land in spam.

Case 02 In production

Workshop management system for 4Power Club

Client
4Power Club
Industry
Car service · Chișinău
Team
6 bays, 5–6 mechanics, 4 service advisors, warehouse

The problem

Information got lost between people: an advisor didn't learn a car was ready if a colleague had checked it in; cars waiting 2–3 weeks for ordered parts were forgotten; the warehouse received parts but told no one; parts ordered for a car that wasn't on site lost their link to it; customers were told their car was ready by hand. Parts were tracked in 1C.

What we built

  • A layer on top of 1C. 1C stays the accounting system; this one runs the process: car statuses, who is responsible, the “part ↔ car” link, notifications.
  • Real-time car board — by day and by status column; urgent cars are highlighted, and you can see how many days each one has been waiting.
  • Parts tracked through the full cycle “car waiting → ordered → in stock → fitted”, plus a separate tab for parts that arrived without a linked car.
  • 1C link — work orders and warehouse receipts come in from 1C on their own, no double entry; a repeated receipt never duplicates a part.
  • “Shop floor screen” — a shared monitor for the mechanics, nothing to install.
  • Thresholds and working hours — the system raises the alarm by itself when a car or a part is stuck longer than it should be.
  • Messages to customers on behalf of the workshop — “your car is ready” and the work order go out automatically once 1C closes the order, with the responsible advisor’s phone number. Staff get Telegram notifications.
  • Appointment calendar as a single queue per day, plus a “Book for repair” button once all parts have arrived.
  • Foreman screen on the phone and a test-drive mark: whoever hands over the keys sees whether the car was test-driven.
  • A daily summary for the owner.
  • Search by plate number, VIN, phone, surname or work order number; staff roles and access rights.

06 / Questions

The questions people hesitate to ask

01How much does it cost, and what drives the price?

Scope: how many processes, integrations and users, and whether you need ads and a website. After the first review I give you a ballpark; after the audit, the exact price and scope. The price is fixed before we start.

02How long will it take?

The audit takes 1–2 weeks. I give the build timeline after the audit: a single job can go live quickly, a large system is built in stages — and you see first results along the way.

03Do you work with 1C and my CRM?

Yes. 1C through its REST API, as in the 4Power Club system. CRMs and other tools — if they have an API or a data export; we check that during the review.

04What happens if you disappear?

The code, servers and access are yours from day one, along with the documentation. Any developer can maintain and extend the system.

05Do you work under a contract?

Yes, always under a written contract: what we will do, the timeline, the price and how payment works.
