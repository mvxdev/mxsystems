1. Who processes the data

The website mxsystems.md belongs to MX Systems studio (Chișinău, Republic of Moldova). The person responsible for data processing is Maxim Lazarev (private individual), Chișinău, Republic of Moldova.

For any questions about your data, write to mvxrev@gmail.com.

2. What data we receive

  • When you contact us via Telegram, WhatsApp, email or phone — your name, contact details and whatever you tell us about your project. The site has no forms that send data.
  • Analytics (only with your consent) — Google Analytics 4: which pages you opened, device and browser type, approximate location. IP addresses are anonymised.
  • Advertising (only with your consent) — Meta Pixel: your visit and actions on the site, to measure how well ads on Facebook and Instagram work.
  • Essential settings — your chosen language and your cookie choice. They are stored only in your browser (localStorage) and are not sent anywhere.

3. Why, and on what basis

  • To reply to your request and prepare an offer — steps taken at your request before entering into a contract.
  • To perform the contract, if we start working together.
  • To understand which parts of the site are useful and to measure ads — only on the basis of your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

Correspondence — as long as it is needed for communication and performing the contract, and for the periods required by law. Analytics data — within the periods listed in the table above.

Processing is carried out in accordance with Law of the Republic of Moldova No. 195/2024 on personal data protection and, for visitors from the EU, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).
